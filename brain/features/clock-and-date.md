# Clock and date

- **Clock**: thin Outfit digits. Each digit that changes breaks into stardust that streams into the
  next, which lands heavy and thins back to a hairline; on the hour the whole time takes off. The
  colon beats with the seconds, a hairline fills across the minute. Between minutes one of six
  moments plays (glint, breathing, wave, orbiting colon, lifting dust, comet). The glow takes the
  focused app's accent. Format: System, 24-hour or 12-hour.
- **Date**: stars twinkle in its letters, a sheen drifts through them, and now and then a trace of
  light, a ripple or a constellation plays; when the date turns, the letters dissolve and condense.
- Both sample glyph strokes through `Glyphs` and read the time through `Wall`.
